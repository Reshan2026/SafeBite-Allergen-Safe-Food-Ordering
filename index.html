How SafeBite works

  1. Tell us your allergies

    Choose from nine common allergens. It is saved on your device.

  2. Every dish is checked

    Each menu item is compared with your profile as you browse.

  3. Dangerous items are blocked

    Confirmed matches cannot be added. Uncertain items ask you to review first.

  4. Check again before you pay

    Your cart is validated once more at checkout.

What our safety labels mean

✔ Safe based on available information

None of your allergens are in the verified ingredients. This is not a guarantee of zero risk.

⚠ Caution

The dish may contain an allergen from shared equipment, was changed, or has missing allergen information. You can review and decide.

⛔ Danger

The dish is confirmed to contain an allergen from your profile. It cannot be ordered.

Missing information is never treated as safe. When data is unavailable, we show “Allergen information unavailable” and let you contact the restaurant.
